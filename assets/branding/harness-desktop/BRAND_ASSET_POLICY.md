# HarnessKit brand asset policy

The user-approved kit-frame artwork at `source/harness-desktop-cabinet.png` is the project-owned HarnessKit application
icon authority.

- Approved source decision: 2026-08-30; transparent-surface correction: 2026-08-31; sparkle removal: 2026-09-01.
- Approved source SHA-256: `c74519996e2ddefd56a77cb2c17bf6cc38a8ed954966c7a17c63a74bc93b9c5f`.
- Approved source format: `1254×1254` RGBA PNG, preserved as original Git-managed bytes. The outer light canvas is
  transparent; the approved artwork's RGB channels are unchanged.
- Approved visual identity: blue-black rounded square, open muted-indigo bracket frame, and central warm-ivory
  connected-node mark. No decorative corner sparkle is part of the approved artwork.
- Approved operation: deterministic resize and platform-container conversion only. Transparency is part of the
  canonical source, not a per-platform artwork transform.
- The approved composition, colors, mark geometry, lighting and background must not be redrawn, recolored, cropped or
  replaced in derived outputs.
- Derived application icons must remain bound to `derivation-manifest.json` and its exact output hashes.
- This policy covers the HarnessKit application brand only. It does not grant rights to third-party tool marks.
